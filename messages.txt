Linera is the first blockchain infrastructure optimized for real-time applications. 
Linera’s unique architecture lets users interact directly with small chains of blocks, called microchains,
allowing applications to read and write onchain data with the lowest latency and highest security possible.
Now, Linera advances beyond today’s L2s by decentralizing even further.
Linera's approach, based on microchains, allows users to own their own personal chain.
providing greater control over performance and security.
Whether proving the origin of your art or switching social platforms, 
Linera is building a truly user-owned future at the vanguard.
We see a future where Web3 is not only mainstream but is powered by infrastructure that is as agile and scalable as the Internet itself.”
Mathieu founded Linera with the vision to enable real-time decentralized interactions to scale seamlessly with user demand.
In 2021, he founded Linera to do just this. He holds a PhD in cryptographic protocols and has extensive experience building distributed systems. 
At Meta, Mathieu contributed to the Libra/Novi project as an early research engineer.
At that time he also worked on the FastPay protocol,
an academic initiative laying the groundwork for Linera’s architecture.
His research, including the Zef protocol, continues to drive performance innovations at Linera.
